### velvet
  
noun. Verbascum thapsus ;= FLANNEL LEAF =VELVET MULLEIN  
> VERBASCUM THAPSUS, a weed of poor land has many popular names. In New Zealand it is called native tobacco, velvet, flannel leaf and golden rod. *(Nov 22, 1974,Dominion,5)*  
> Most of the [Upper Waitaki] valley grew wonderful wheat,  particularly velvet wheat. It still does, although not much, if any, velvet is grown. In 1915, we had a good crop of velvet which grew to a good height. *(1980,Land of Munros, The,91)*

### velvet
  
verb. to collect velvet from a deer.  
> Hawke's Bay Today Oct 31. 15 The National Velveting Standards Body, which runs the certification scheme, has sent out a blunt warning that farmers who do not comply risk being suspended, thus losing the right to velvet their own deer. Any velveter who does not complete one or more of the requirements by the due dates will not be allowed to velvet next season. *(2002)*  
> So we got out of velveting our stags... *(May 16, 2003,Dominion Post,C5)*
